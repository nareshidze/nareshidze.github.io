Nika Areshidze

Department of Mathematics
University of California, Irvine

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of California, Irvine, advised by Professor Paata Ivanisvili.

My interests lie broadly in analysis, and I am especially interested in harmonic analysis, functional inequalities, discrete and Gaussian analysis.
